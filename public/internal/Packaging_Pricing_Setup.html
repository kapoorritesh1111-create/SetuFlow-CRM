What to do on each page

Admin → Reference Library

1. Keep names consistent

Confirm the materials and finishes your team actually uses, such as PET / MET PET / PE, Matte, Zipper and Tear Notch.

Done when: the names you need are available to pick in Pricing Templates.
Admin → Packaging Service Families

2. Create what the buyer sees

Create a family such as Stand-Up Pouches or Flat Bottom Pouches. Set the default unit to PCS when you sell individual pouches.

Done when: sales can identify the correct packaging family.
Admin → Packaging Pricing Templates

3. Enter the pricing rules

For materials, enter the rate in the template currency per m². Add finish/add-on charges as per pouch/piece or per m². Add quantity tiers, setup/pre-press charges, waste, rush and lead time.

Important: the currency box is money. Material rate basis and add-on basis are shown next to the amount.
Live Preview → Test your price

4. Prove the result

Select the material and tick the finishes/add-ons you want included in the test. The preview uses the same pricing engine as Quote Builder.

Done when: adding Zipper/Matte changes the price exactly as expected and the final per-pouch/piece price matches a known manual quote.
