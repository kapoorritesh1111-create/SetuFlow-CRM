Special Production Rules

The three sizes where production differs from the standard integrated route.

110 × 170: Sales must choose Solid color bottom or Logo / text / artwork. This is the only size where Sales is asked a bottom question.
260 × 340: always split body + bottom gusset. No Sales question.
280 × 360: always split body + bottom gusset. No Sales question.
Split protection: gusset inherits the main wastage band but does not receive duplicate margin, pouching or zipper.
