Packaging family structure

Nothing should look “missing” when it is not used

  • PET and Clear PET are not part of the Matte + Foil sample. Their ₹/kg rates are required only when those other constructions are selected.
  • The sample directly uses BOPP, MetPET, PE, adhesive and zipper rates.
  • The sheet’s Extra = 0 field does not feed a pricing formula, so it is not treated as a price control.
  • Freight is deliberately not added until Stark provides the charging rule.
