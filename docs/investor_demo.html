The problem · today

A single export quote takes 3 days, 4 tools, 17 emails.

Import-export sales teams don't fail because they lack a CRM. They fail because the work isn't pipeline management — it's commercial execution under uncertainty.

Pricing depends on FX. Freight depends on port and container. Documents depend on country. Approvals depend on margin. And every one of those lives in a different spreadsheet, WhatsApp thread, or PDF on someone's desktop.

  1. 01

    The card from the trade fair becomes a Notes screenshot.

    By Tuesday it's lost. By next month the lead is cold.

  2. 02

    The quote is a Word doc with a hand-typed FX rate.

    Margin is wrong by the time the buyer replies.

  3. 03

    The approval is a Slack message to the boss.

    No record. No version. No accountability.

  4. 04

    The order is an email forward to ops.

    Documents missing. Compliance discovered at customs.

Why us · the wedge

Three things only Setu Flow does — and why each is hard to copy.

01 · Product moat

A trade-native quote builder.

Pricing isn't a number. It's basis × currency × FX-as-of × freight profile × container fit × margin policy. Setu Flow models all of it natively, recomputes on every change, and produces a clean buyer-ready quote — with the audit trail of every input intact.

Why hard to copy
Years of trade-ops domain logic in schema · 40+ tables · per-port freight profiles · multi-currency FX resolution. Not a feature — an operating model.
02 · Trust moat

A trust layer baked into the flow.

Approval gates, immutable lock at send, full audit trail, AI that drafts but never sends. Built for the compliance officer who has to defend a deal eighteen months later — not the rep who just wants to close. Enterprise-grade trust as a default, not a feature flag.

Why hard to copy
Generic CRMs would have to re-architect to bolt this on without breaking pipeline UX. Trust is a foundation, not a layer you paint on top.
03 · Distribution moat

A two-way contact loop.

Inbound capture (OCR, vCard, RFQ doc) plus an outward My Card / public QR page that turns every business card you hand out at a trade fair into an inbound capture form. Quote requests land as leads with full context — no friction, no re-typing.

Why hard to copy
Every customer becomes a distribution channel. Each card scanned by their buyer creates a free lead surfaced in our network. Compounds with usage.
Business model

Per-seat SaaS · land at $299/mo · expand to $2k+ as the trade team grows.

Trade teams are organized in tight pods (5–15 sales, ops, and compliance roles per office). Setu Flow lands inside a pod, proves quote velocity, and expands across markets and verticals inside the same parent company. Pricing rewards depth, not breadth.

Add-ons — country compliance packs, custom freight profiles, and AI assist credits — give us a clean second revenue lane without breaking the per-seat model.
