# Setu Guru — Packaging Compliance Research Library

## Purpose

This library helps Packaging users identify the evidence that may be required before quote approval, production release, customer claims, or dispatch. It is guidance only. Setu Guru must not approve compliance, waive a requirement, certify a material, or publish a legal/environmental claim.

## Required context before research

- Destination country and market
- Product being packed
- Direct or indirect product contact
- Material structure and layer composition
- Ink, adhesive, coating, and finish
- Filling, sealing, storage, temperature, and shelf-life conditions
- Recycled-content or environmental claims
- Pharmaceutical, cosmetic, child-resistant, or tamper-evident use

## Evidence domains

### Food-contact packaging

Review material declarations, overall migration testing, specific migration testing where applicable, intended-use conditions, and supplier traceability. Supplier declarations do not replace application-specific or destination-specific review.

### Inks, coatings, and adhesives

Collect ink and adhesive compliance declarations, low-migration statements where applicable, and evidence that the selected system is suitable for the substrate, curing process, and packed product.

### Restricted substances and heavy metals

Collect restricted-substances declarations, heavy-metals declarations, and certificates of analysis where relevant. Validate current market requirements before relying on a declaration.

### Recyclability, recycled content, and compostability

Environmental claims require substantiation. Recyclability depends on the complete structure and local collection/reprocessing system. Recycled-content and compostability claims require appropriate evidence and certification scope.

### Paper and fiber claims

FSC or PEFC claims require valid chain-of-custody scope and correct trademark/claim use.

### EPR, packaging waste, and plastic taxes

Obligations vary by market, material, weight, recycled content, importer/producer role, and reporting threshold. Use current official sources and qualified review.

### Regulated packaging formats

Child-resistant, tamper-evident, pharmaceutical, and cosmetic packaging may require compatibility, performance, stability, or certified test evidence. Human compliance approval is mandatory.

### Artwork, labeling, barcodes, and variable data

Maintain approved artwork, dieline, color standard, barcode verification, legal-copy review, and variable-data verification. Setu Guru may identify missing evidence but cannot approve artwork or data.

## Human approval boundaries

- Compliance clearance and waivers
- Environmental or certification claims
- Food-contact or regulated-use suitability
- Artwork legal copy
- Production release
- Customer-facing declarations
- Country-specific legal conclusions

For unstable legal and regulatory questions, Setu Guru must use current official sources and show the retrieval date and market context.
