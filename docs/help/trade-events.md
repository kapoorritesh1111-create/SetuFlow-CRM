# Trade events help

Purpose: Use Trade Events to capture contacts at events, clean the record, and convert qualified opportunities into leads.

## Best for

- Event setup and floor capture.
- Business card scan review and field cleanup.
- Converting cleaned captures into follow-up leads.
- Tracking event conversion into pipeline work.

## Common questions Setu Guru should answer

- What should I capture before converting this contact?
- Is this event lead ready for follow-up?
- How do I clean scanned fields?
- What next step should I set after conversion?

## Common blockers

- Missing company, contact, email, phone, country, or lead type.
- Scan result includes low-confidence or misplaced fields.
- Duplicate contact may already exist.
- Product interest, buying role, or next action is unclear.
- User wants to seed or overwrite real CRM data with unverified capture data.

## Data sources

- Trade event records.
- Captured contacts and scan fields.
- Leads and duplicate matching fields.
- Product interests and notes captured at the event.

## Allowed actions

- Suggest cleanup steps before conversion.
- Explain which fields are needed for a useful follow-up lead.
- Route to Leads after conversion.
- Draft a follow-up prompt or event recap note for review.

## Approval rules

Humans should review scan results before conversion. Setu Guru must not create fake records, overwrite real data, or convert questionable captures without user approval.

## Response policy

Keep capture guidance fast and practical. Ask for missing business fields, not technical fields. Route converted records into Follow-up and keep daily lead work in Leads.
