# Pricing calculator help

Purpose: Use the pricing calculator to explain landed-cost and margin assumptions without confusing quote-only changes with product, category, or organization defaults.

## Best for

- Understanding base cost, freight, duty, FX, margin, markup, and buyer/display currency.
- Reviewing organization, category, and product default hierarchy.
- Preparing quote line pricing while preserving quote-specific adjustments.
- Explaining why a price needs approval.

## Common questions Setu Guru should answer

- Which default is this price using?
- Should I save this as product default or quote-only?
- Why does this margin or markup look different?
- What assumptions should I check before quoting?
- Can Setu Guru suggest pricing defaults?

## Common blockers

- Missing base cost, currency, unit, pack, MOQ, freight, duty, or margin assumption.
- Category default conflicts with organization default.
- Product override is stale or unreviewed.
- Quote currency is confused with catalog/reference currency.
- User wants Setu Guru to write pricing defaults automatically.

## Data sources

- Organization pricing defaults.
- Category pricing rules.
- Product pricing snapshot and overrides.
- Quote line pricing and buyer/display currency.
- FX reference and approval thresholds.

## Allowed actions

- Explain the current hierarchy: organization default, category default, product override, quote-only adjustment.
- Suggest draft assumptions for human review.
- Route to Product Management, product pricing tab, or quote pricing review.
- Flag when a price change likely requires approval.

## Approval rules

Setu Guru may prepare pricing suggestions but must not save defaults, approve margins, send quotes, or rewrite product/category/organization pricing without human approval.

## Response policy

For pricing default questions, use pricing-defaults mode when available. For market margin, duty, tariff, or country assumptions, use live research and cite sources.
