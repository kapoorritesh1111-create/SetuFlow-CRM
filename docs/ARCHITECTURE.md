# Architecture posture

Setu Flow CRM has a stronger architecture than its current investor-proof state might suggest.

## Strong today

- modular domain structure
- clear workflow-oriented library layer
- internal truth dashboard
- governed pricing posture
- documentation discipline

## Still partial

- accepted-state proof across quote surfaces
- strongest approval-governed commercial example
- visible order/execution maturity
- live integration evidence

## Architectural principle

The system is designed to preserve commercial continuity, not just store records.

The most important architectural rule remains:

1. products have a base catalog price
2. quotes may override price only with reason
3. override approval must remain visible and enforceable
4. downstream order/contract continuity must preserve commercial truth

## Viewer rule

Architecture must be explainable in business language. The SVG workflows in the DCC exist to make the architecture understandable even to non-technical stakeholders.
