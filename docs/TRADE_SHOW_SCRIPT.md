# Trade-Show Script

## 2-minute version

### Opening
Setu Flow is a trade-focused CRM and execution workspace for import-export teams.

### Core message
Most CRMs stop at contact and pipeline visibility. Setu Flow carries the workflow through:
- lead qualification
- quote control
- contract-backed continuity
- trade readiness
- order execution visibility

### Fast walkthrough
1. Dashboard shows what is delayed, blocked, or urgent.
2. Leads keeps buyer or supplier context visible.
3. Quotes keeps revision, handoff, and risk posture visible.
4. Orders exposes contract continuity, freight readiness, compliance posture, and dispatch evidence.
5. AI and Integrations stay bounded by the same governed workflow truth.

### Close
Setu Flow is designed to become the operating hub for importer-exporter teams, not just another generic CRM.

## 5-minute version

### Problem framing
Trade teams lose time and trust when opportunity data, quoting, approvals, and execution readiness are scattered.

### What Setu Flow does differently
- action-first dashboard
- explicit pipeline movement and next action clarity
- quote-to-contract-to-order continuity
- trade workflow signals embedded in the working surfaces
- explainable and bounded AI guidance
- governed integrations with validation, replay visibility, and safe outbound sync posture

### Suggested booth flow
- show dashboard first
- move into leads and pipeline
- show quotes risk posture
- show order delay posture and dispatch evidence
- end on governed AI and integrations visibility

## Talking points
- built for import-export operations, not generic SaaS theater
- visibility into trade readiness before execution slips
- structured handoff between commercial and operational stages
- AI used for prioritization and operator review, not hype
- integrations treated as controlled infrastructure, not magic sync

## What not to claim
- do not claim live provider certification unless it exists
- do not position mock connectors as finished production integrations
- do not use internal readiness language in external demos
- do not imply autonomous approvals, autonomous dispatch, or weakened override controls
