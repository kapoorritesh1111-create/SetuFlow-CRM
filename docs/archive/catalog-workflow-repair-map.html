1. What is architecturally wrong today

Current broken mental model
/products is mature, but /catalog also shows a Products tab and product list. /price-lists packages pricing, but it does not clearly route back to the Products & Pricing source of truth.
Correct mental model
/products owns products, variants, readiness, and product pricing. /price-lists packages that pricing. /catalog sends and tracks buyer shares.
Duplicate product workspace→Fragmented pricing checks→Buyer sees price-on-request incorrectly→Quote draft misses canonical price

2. Current wrong workflow vs corrected target workflow

Current wrong flow

/catalog→Products tab→Local price assumptions→Share warning
Catalog feels like a second product database. Validation and KPIs can drift away from /products and /price-lists.

Correct target flow

/products→Pricing drawer→/price-lists→Share Wizard→Buyer Room→Quote Draft
The workflow starts where pricing already exists. Every later surface must read the same product/price-list resolver.
The target path is intentionally linear for review: product truth first, packaging second, sharing third, conversion last.

3. What each page owns after repair

/products
Owns product catalog, product details, variants, readiness, EXW/FOB/CIF/DDP, Distributor, Retail, pricing drawer, and quote handoff.
/price-lists
Owns market, buyer segment, incoterm, MOQ, tiers, selected products, and source labels pulled from product pricing.
/catalog
Owns Share Catalog CTA, Shared Links, buyer engagement, analytics, Setu Guru share recommendations, and quote conversion entry points.

4. Screen-by-screen target mockups

Screen 1 — /products is the starting point

Click target to add selected product(s) into a price list directly from the mature workspace.

Screen 2 — Product pricing drawer already has canonical pricing

Price list packaging must consume this pricing, not ask the seller to re-enter it.

Screen 3 — /price-lists packages product pricing

Buyer Segment must be buyer taxonomy, not product categories. Source label tells the seller where the price came from.

Screen 4 — Share Catalog Wizard sends shares; it does not own pricing

The wizard validates coverage after a price list is selected. Product readiness belongs on Step 1; pricing belongs on Step 2 and Review.

Screen 5 — Buyer Room shows selected price-list pricing

Price-on-request is shown only when the shared resolver cannot resolve list or product pricing.

Screen 6 — Shared Links / Quote Conversion

Quote draft must use the same resolver path as buyer room and PDF, not only price-list items.

6. Repair chunks after approval

Chunk A — Workflow map only
This page. No behavior changes. User reviews target workflow.
Chunk B — Navigation + Catalog Hub cleanup
Make /catalog the sharing command center and add visible paths from /products and /price-lists.
Chunk C — Shared pricing resolver
Create one resolver for price list items, product pricing workspace, pricing fields/rules, and price-on-request fallback.
Chunk D — Price List auto-fill
Auto-fill Base Unit Price and source label from selected product + incoterm + currency.
Chunk E — Share Wizard warnings
Step 1 readiness only; Step 2 and Review pricing coverage after price list selection.
Chunk F/G — Taxonomy + KPIs
Separate buyer segments from product categories and align Catalog KPIs to canonical data sources.
