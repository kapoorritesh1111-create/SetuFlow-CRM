Sprint 31 · Product-wide

Lead capture now prepares a customer-safe intro.

Quick Lead / Capture Lead saves the lead first, then records intro communications without blocking the workflow.

After save

Flow

Create lead, assign pipeline/stage, schedule follow-up, then prepare intro records.

Safe content

No internal notes

Customer text uses structured fields only: event, booth, org category, product/request, contact details, follow-up, and vCard.

Channels

Email + WhatsApp

Email is queued/logged when email exists. WhatsApp is drafted/logged unless a live provider is connected.

Customer context

  • Where we met: event/source label
  • Who they met: sender profile and organization
  • What the org sells: organization category
  • What was discussed: product/category/request field
  • How to reconnect: sender email, phone, website, and vCard link
  • When follow-up happens: scheduled follow-up time or “soon”
