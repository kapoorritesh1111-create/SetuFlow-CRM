# Pass 13 — Pilot Operations Checklist

Use this checklist during a controlled pilot. Do **not** use or mutate the frozen golden record `Q-00025`; pilot evidence must come from a new proof record.

## Pre-pilot operator checklist

- Confirm workspace owner and backup owner.
- Confirm admin access and role assignments.
- Confirm organization settings, reference lists, products, pricing, and approval threshold.
- Confirm known non-claims are understood by the pilot customer.
- Confirm support owner, incident channel, and escalation contacts.

## Pilot-day checklist

- Admin logs in.
- Invite pilot users.
- Confirm product/pricing setup.
- Create first pilot lead.
- Build and send first quote.
- Capture quote acceptance evidence.
- Generate contract/order.
- Capture signing, document, dispatch, and audit evidence when available.

## Daily pilot operations checklist

- Review new leads, quotes, approvals, documents, and order status.
- Review audit logs for every revenue-path action.
- Track support tickets, failed imports, blocked orders, and manual workarounds.
- Capture screenshots/exports for evidence.

## End-of-week review checklist

- Review activation metrics and customer feedback.
- Review unresolved blockers and support tickets.
- Decide whether the pilot remains conditional, expands, or pauses.

## Escalation checklist

- Sev 1: customer blocked from revenue workflow. Escalate to technical owner immediately.
- Sev 2: degraded workflow with workaround. Escalate during same business day.
- Sev 3: documentation or help request. Track and resolve in normal support cadence.

## Non-claims

This checklist does not prove a completed pilot, live signed contract, dispatch, WAF, monitoring, or production-scale readiness. It defines the operational evidence that must be collected.
